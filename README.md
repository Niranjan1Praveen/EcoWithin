## 🌱 A Brief of the Prototype
![Landing Page](designs/LandingPage.png)  

**EchoWithin** is a full-stack **voice-first AI web application** that enables users to communicate in real time with an emotionally aware AI agent speaking in a natural human accent.  

The platform encourages users to **express themselves freely through voice**, after which the AI analyzes emotional cues such as tone, pauses, sentiment, and conversational patterns to generate **personal emotional insights**.  

Designed as part of a happiness-focused academic project, EchoWithin prioritizes **self-awareness, reflection, and emotional literacy** rather than productivity or diagnosis.  

Unlike text-based chatbots, EchoWithin leverages **real-time speech interaction** to capture deeper emotional signals and present them back to users in a meaningful, reflective manner.

---

## 🧩 Modules Overview

### 🎙️ 1. Real-Time Voice AI Conversation
![Voice Interaction](designs/VoiceInteraction.png)  

A **live voice communication module** that allows users to talk naturally with an AI agent.  
- Real-time speech-to-text and text-to-speech using **Vapi**  
- Accent-adaptive AI voice (calm, warm, human-like)  
- Dynamic response pacing based on user tone and energy  
- Low-latency, interruption-free conversations  

> 💬 This module forms the emotional core of the application.

---

### 🧠 2. Post-Conversation Emotion Interpretation Engine
![Emotion Insights](designs/EmotionInsights.png)  

After each conversation, the AI analyzes both **spoken content and vocal patterns** to extract emotional insights.  
- Identifies dominant emotions (calm, anxious, hopeful, reflective, etc.)  
- Detects emotional fluctuations during the session  
- Highlights recurring themes and emotional triggers  
- Generates empathetic, non-judgmental reflections  

**Sample Insight Output**
> “You spoke with confidence while discussing goals, but your tone softened when talking about personal expectations, suggesting a desire for reassurance.”

> 🌿 Designed to promote awareness, not evaluation.

---

### 🧱 3. Bento Grid Experience (Frontend UX)
![Bento Grid](designs/BentoGrid.png)  

A **modern Bento-style grid layout** presenting key features and insights:  
- Voice sessions overview  
- Emotional summaries  
- Reflection highlights  
- Progress over time  

**Design Highlights**
- Glassmorphism cards  
- Soft gradients and rounded layouts  
- Micro-interactions for emotional feedback  

> 🧩 Enhances clarity and calmness through thoughtful UI design.

---

### 🎨 4. 3D Hero Experience
![3D Hero](designs/Hero3D.png)  

An immersive **3D GLB model** placed in the hero section to represent the AI presence.
